- SpoolToCompleted: spool → claimed → completed, end to end.
- RecoverAfterHardStop: at-least-once — a hard-killed runner's
  interrupted job is re-queued and completed by the next one.
- LockSingleExecutor: the second runner cannot take the
  per-directory file lock.
- TelemetryJSONL: the runner's telemetry reaches the JSONL leg.
